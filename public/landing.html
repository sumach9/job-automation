What it does
Find. Score. Apply. Repeat.

Every step of the job search loop — automated, tracked, and improving with every run.

🔍
Multi-platform search
Scrapes LinkedIn (Apify), Google Jobs, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Robert Half, Experis, Volt, and 14 more staffing agencies simultaneously.
⭐
AI fit scoring
Every job scored 0–5 against your resume and target roles. Only high-fit jobs get applied to — no wasted applications.
🤖
Auto-apply
Playwright navigates live application forms. Groq's Llama 3.3-70B reads every field and maps your profile to it, then submits.
✨
SAM Chrome extension
AI assistant button injected next to every textarea on LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and 7 more platforms.
📊
Pipeline CRM
Kanban board tracks every application from discovery to offer. Daily email digest and Google Sheets sync built in.
🎯
AI mock interviews
Streaming AI interviewer tailored to each job you applied to. Voice input, real-time scoring, and performance feedback after every session.

AI feature suite
Beyond auto-apply

Four novel AI features built on top of the core automation.

👻
Ghosting Oracle
TF.js binary classifier trained on your own application history. Predicts which applications will go dark before they do.
TensorFlow.js · In-browser ML
🎯
Interview War Room
Streaming AI interviewer (Groq Llama 3.3-70B) tailored to the job you applied to. Voice input + live scoring after every session.
SSE · Voice · Groq
🌌
Semantic Job Galaxy
Canvas 2D map of your job search results. Jobs placed by seniority and domain with physics repulsion. Hover to inspect, click to apply.
Canvas 2D · Physics
🤖
Autopilot Apply
Playwright scans any live job application URL, extracts all form fields from the DOM, and AI maps your profile to each one.
Playwright · Groq
